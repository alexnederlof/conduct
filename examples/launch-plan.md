# A quieter kind of launch

A first look at Fieldwork — a small, thoughtful space for teams to turn scattered ideas into something worth sharing.

---

## The idea

Good work rarely arrives fully formed. It starts in a conversation, a half-written note, a thought someone almost didn’t share. **Fieldwork gives those beginnings a place to grow.**

We’re building a shared workspace that feels less like managing a project and more like working around the same table. A place to collect what matters, make connections, and move an idea forward together.

> Make room for the thinking. The good work will follow.

## Who we’re building for

Small teams with big ideas and too many open tabs. Designers, founders, researchers, and anyone who believes the best part of making something is figuring it out together.

- **The curious team.** Gathering references, asking better questions, and finding a direction.
- **The independent maker.** Moving between a rough thought and a finished thing.
- **The thoughtful collaborator.** Leaving useful feedback that makes the next version better.

## A launch in three chapters

| Chapter         | What happens                             | When      |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Plant the seed  | A personal invitation to 20 teams        | Week 1    |
| Find the rhythm | Listen, learn, and refine the essentials | Weeks 2–3 |
| Open the door   | Share what we’ve learned with everyone   | Week 4    |

### Start with a conversation

We’ll invite twenty teams to use Fieldwork on a real project. No guided demo, no carefully staged workflow. Just their work, their questions, and a direct line to us.

The first version should support shared notes, a simple canvas, and inline feedback. Everything else can wait until we know what people actually need.

### Let the work tell the story

Instead of a long list of features, we’ll share three stories about what people made. Each story should be specific enough to feel real and simple enough to spark an idea.

## What success looks like

Our goal is to reach 10,000 signups in the first month. We’ll also watch how often teams return, how many ideas become shared projects, and what people tell a friend about us.

The most useful signal might be the simplest: someone opens Fieldwork on a Monday morning because they want to, not because they have to.
